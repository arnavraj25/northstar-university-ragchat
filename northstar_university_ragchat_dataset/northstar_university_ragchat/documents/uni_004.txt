NORTHSTAR UNIVERSITY
Course Registration Policy
Document ID: UNI-004
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 1 July 2026
Status: Official fictional policy

1. Registration period
For the 2026–27 academic year, the standard course registration window for the first semester is 1–10 July 2026. The standard window for the second semester is 2–10 January 2027.

2. Add or drop a course
Students may request to add or drop a course through the student portal during the first five calendar days after the relevant semester begins. Approval depends on seat availability, prerequisites, and the program's credit requirements.

3. Prerequisites
Students must satisfy the prerequisites listed in the official course catalogue before registering for a course. The registration system may block enrollment when a prerequisite is missing.

4. Credit load
The normal semester load is 18–24 credits. Requests to register for fewer than 18 or more than 24 credits must be reviewed by the program coordinator.

5. Registration help
Students who encounter a portal error should save a screenshot and contact the Registrar's Office. They should not share their password with staff or other students.
