Library and Campus Facilities Guide

1. Main library hours
During teaching weeks, the main library is open Monday through Friday from 08:00 to 20:00 and Saturday from 09:00 to 16:00. It is closed on Sundays and published university holidays.

2. Borrowing books
Undergraduate students may borrow up to four books at a time for 14 calendar days. Books may be renewed once if no other user has reserved them.

3. Computer laboratories
The general computer laboratories are open Monday through Friday from 09:00 to 18:00 during teaching weeks. Access may be restricted during scheduled classes, maintenance, or examinations.

4. Student help desk
The campus help desk is located in the Student Services Building and operates Monday through Friday from 09:00 to 17:00, excluding published university holidays.

5. Accessibility and equipment
Students who need accessible study arrangements or specialized equipment should contact Student Services at least three working days before the intended visit. Availability must be confirmed by the office.
