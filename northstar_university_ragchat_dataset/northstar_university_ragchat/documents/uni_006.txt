1. Merit scholarship eligibility
New undergraduate students with a score of at least 90% in the qualifying examination may apply for the Northstar Merit Scholarship. Meeting the threshold permits an application but does not guarantee an award.

2. Scholarship value
The standard Northstar Merit Scholarship covers 25% of undergraduate tuition for one academic year. It does not automatically cover accommodation, meals, transport, or other personal expenses.

3. Application documents
Applicants must submit the scholarship form, qualifying examination result, proof of admission, and a student identity document through the scholarship portal by the deadline shown on that portal.

4. Renewal
Renewal is assessed annually. Students must maintain a cumulative GPA of at least 3.5 on the university's 4.0 scale and complete all required credits for the academic year. Renewal remains subject to verification and available funding.

5. Other financial aid
This document describes only the Northstar Merit Scholarship. It does not specify eligibility or amounts for need-based grants, emergency loans, or external scholarships. Contact the Student Support Office for those programs.
