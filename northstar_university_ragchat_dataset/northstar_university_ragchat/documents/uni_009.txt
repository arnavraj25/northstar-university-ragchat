Student Code of Conduct
Document ID: UNI-009
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 1 July 2026
Status: Official fictional policy

1. Respectful conduct
Students are expected to treat other students, staff, visitors, and campus workers respectfully and must not engage in harassment, threats, or discriminatory conduct.

2. Academic integrity
Plagiarism, fabrication of academic work, unauthorized collaboration, and use of prohibited materials during assessments are academic integrity violations.

3. Reporting a concern
A student may report a concern to the Student Conduct Office or through the official student portal. Reports should include a clear description of the incident and any relevant evidence. Reports are handled confidentially to the extent possible under the review process.

4. Review process
The university reviews reported concerns before deciding whether a policy violation occurred. Students involved in a review will be informed of the applicable process and given an opportunity to respond.

5. Possible outcomes
Depending on the findings and severity, outcomes may include an educational warning, required training, a formal reprimand, or suspension under the university's disciplinary procedure. No outcome is automatic; decisions depend on the case.
