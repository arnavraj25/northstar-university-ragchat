NORTHSTAR UNIVERSITY
Grading and Assessment Policy
Document ID: UNI-003
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 1 July 2026
Status: Official fictional policy

1. Assessment structure
Unless a course outline states otherwise, a course is assessed through 40 marks of continuous assessment and 60 marks in the end-semester examination, for a total of 100 marks.

2. Passing requirement
A student must earn at least 40 marks out of 100 overall and at least 24 out of 60 in the end-semester examination to pass a course.

3. Grade bands
The default grade bands are:
A: 85–100
B: 70–84
C: 55–69
D: 40–54
F: below 40 or failure to meet the minimum end-semester examination requirement.

4. Course-specific rules
An approved course outline may define a different assessment structure if it is published to students before assessment begins. Students should consult their current course outline for any approved variation.

5. Grade questions
Questions about a grade should first be directed to the course instructor. Requests to correct a clerical error may be submitted under the procedure in UNI-002.
