NORTHSTAR UNIVERSITY
Examination Rules
Document ID: UNI-002
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 1 July 2026
Status: Official fictional policy

1. Examination eligibility
To sit the regular end-semester examination for a course, a student must meet the attendance requirement in UNI-001 and complete any course-specific assessment requirements announced by the instructor.

2. Identification
Students must bring their university student identity card to an in-person examination. A student who has forgotten the card should report to the examination supervisor before the examination begins and follow the supervisor's instructions.

3. Arrival time
Students should arrive at least 15 minutes before the scheduled start time. Entry after the examination has started is subject to the examination supervisor's decision.

4. Academic integrity
Unauthorized notes, communication devices used for assistance, or collaboration during an individual examination are prohibited. Suspected violations are referred for review under the Student Code of Conduct (UNI-009).

5. Results and review
Results are published through the student portal when approved for release. A student who believes a mark was recorded incorrectly may request a clerical review through the Academic Office within seven calendar days of result publication. A clerical review checks recording and calculation; it is not a re-evaluation of academic judgment.
