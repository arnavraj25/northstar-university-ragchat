Tuition Fee and Payment Policy
Document ID: UNI-005
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 1 July 2026
Status: Official fictional policy

1. Undergraduate tuition
The standard undergraduate tuition fee is INR 85,000 per semester for the 2026–27 academic year. Program-specific fees, if any, are listed in the student's admission letter.

2. Payment deadlines
For the 2026–27 academic year, the standard payment deadline is 15 July 2026 for the first semester and 15 January 2027 for the second semester.

3. Late payment charge
Unless an approved extension or documented exception applies, a late payment charge of INR 500 is applied after the deadline, followed by INR 100 for each subsequent calendar day that the balance remains unpaid.

4. Payment methods
Students can pay through the university student portal using the payment methods displayed there. Students should retain the transaction receipt until the payment appears in their account statement.

5. Fee refunds
This policy does not define hostel fee refunds, travel reimbursements, or refunds for optional extracurricular activities. Students seeking information on those topics should contact the Finance Office.
